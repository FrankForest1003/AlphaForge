# AlphaForge Research Library

The ten papers used by the final literature review are archived under
`papers/AlphaForge_论文_已按年份期刊题目命名/`. Files follow the convention
`year_venue_title.pdf` so the publication year, venue, and title remain
auditable.

## Coverage

- 10 peer-reviewed research papers in total;
- 7 papers published from 2023 to 2025;
- peer-reviewed work from RFS, AAAI, Artificial Intelligence Review, ACM
  ICAIF, ACM WWW, NeurIPS, Journal of Comparative Economics, Journal of
  Computational Finance, and Findings of EMNLP;
- two survey papers covering deep-learning stock-market research and finance
  LLM agents;
- topics spanning asset-pricing ML, risk-aware portfolio management,
  explainable stock prediction, financial multi-agent systems, backtest
  overfitting, and game-based financial education.

The supplied `论文要求核对说明.md` is kept next to the PDFs and maps the
collection to the course literature requirements. The final literature review
records the current approaches, strengths, limitations, research gaps, and
AlphaForge design opportunities derived from this corpus.

## Repository policy

These PDFs are research inputs, not runtime assets. Application containers must
not copy `docs/research/papers/`, and every paper used in slides or reports must
be cited in the final bibliography.
