# AlphaForge Research Library

The nine supplied papers are archived under
`papers/AlphaForge_论文_已按年份期刊题目命名/`. The original filenames are retained
so the year, venue, and title remain auditable.

## Coverage

- 9 research papers in total;
- 7 papers published from 2023 to 2025;
- peer-reviewed work from RFS, EJOR, Artificial Intelligence Review, ACL,
  ACM ICAIF, ACM WWW, and Findings of EMNLP;
- two survey papers covering deep-learning stock-market research and finance
  LLM agents;
- topics spanning asset pricing ML, time-series prediction, explainable stock
  prediction, multi-agent generation/testing, and financial trading agents.

The supplied `论文要求核对说明.md` is kept next to the PDFs. A later literature
matrix should record research question, data, method, evaluation, limitation,
and the exact AlphaForge design decision supported by each paper.

## Repository policy

These PDFs are research inputs, not runtime assets. Application containers must
not copy `docs/research/papers/`, and every paper used in slides or reports must
be cited in the final bibliography.
